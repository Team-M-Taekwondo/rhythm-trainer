# Poomsae audio

Team-wide poomsae audio clips live here and are referenced by `../data/clips.json`.

Suggested layout:
- `color/<id>.<ext>` — color-belt poomsae (Taegeuk 1–8)
- `black/<division>/<id>.<ext>` — black-belt, e.g. `black/senior/9.mp3`

Long provides the edited audio files (from his video editor) and the tension
timings; Claude drops them here and adds the matching entry in `data/clips.json`.
